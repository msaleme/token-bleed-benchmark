## Permitted summary

In Token-Bleed R5's named local configuration, compact governed selection used substantially
fewer prompt tokens and achieved higher F1 than raw full-context stuffing. It did not clear the
preregistered three-times prompt-token ceiling against the lexical baseline. The public result is
therefore a bounded runtime characterization, not a production ROI result, a cross-model claim,
or evidence that governed context always earns its complexity.

## Publication angles

### Technical reader

**Working thesis:** A benchmark needs both a raw-full comparator and a cheap selective baseline.
Without the lexical baseline, a low-token improvement can be mistaken for an economic win.

**Required links:** frozen contract, immutable release, ACE decision pack, and R5 result.

### Video reader

comparators in under 90 seconds, then link to the technical record. Do not quote the figure
without the lexical-cost limitation in the same asset.
